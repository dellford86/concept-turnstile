# 02 — Fan Jobs-to-be-Done

Core motivations, desired progress, and critical anxieties of stadium fans.

## Core Job Statement

> "When I arrive at a home game, help me make a fast, informed attendance prediction so I feel more invested in the live event and have a reason to return next game."

## Specific Fan Jobs

### Functional
Notice game before kickoff, make a plausible guess without research fatigue, and track historic prediction accuracy over the season.

### Emotional
Feel an acute surge of excitement during the 3rd quarter official attendance reveal and celebrate streak milestones.

### Social
Rival friends in neighboring sections, share accuracy percentiles on social, and prove sports-IQ bragging rights.

## Active Fan Anxieties

- Missing the 30-sec entry window before kickoff locks.
- Unclear mechanics on how tie-breakers for exact-match draws work.
- Feeling defeated with zero payoff after repeatedly missing by <100 fans.

---

## Design checklist for new components/features/pages

- [ ] Does it serve the core job (fast, informed prediction → investment in the live event → reason to return)?
- [ ] Functional: does it stay low-friction and avoid research fatigue?
- [ ] Emotional: does it build toward (or pay off) the reveal-moment surge, not dilute it?
- [ ] Social: does it give fans something to rival, compare, or share?
- [ ] Does it resolve, rather than introduce, one of the known anxieties (entry window, tie-breakers, near-miss payoff)?
