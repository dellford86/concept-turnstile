# 03 — Organization Goals

What the franchise gains. Turning attention into business assets.

## Critical Organizational Jobs

### App Retention & Daily Actives
Provide a highly recurring, non-intrusive reason to launch the official team app inside the stadium boundary.

### First-Party Data
Verify ticketing arrival status, linking in-venue geo-location records with active digital fan profiles.

### Sponsorship Monetization
Integrate sponsors into high-value Jumbotron countdowns, push notifications, and reveal alerts.

## North Star Metrics

- **42%** — In-Venue Conversion
- **81%** — Repeat Player Rate

---

## Design checklist for new components/features/pages

- [ ] Does it give fans a reason to open the app while in-venue (supports retention/DAU)?
- [ ] Does it capture or reinforce first-party data (verified arrival, geo, fan profile) without violating fan-facing privacy boundaries?
- [ ] Does it create a legitimate sponsorship surface, or at least avoid closing one off?
- [ ] Can its impact be traced back to In-Venue Conversion or Repeat Player Rate?
