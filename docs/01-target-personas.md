# 01 — Target Personas

Who Turnstile is built for. Mapping stadium audience layers.

**Eligibility:** In-venue fans only. One verified entry per home game. Free to play.

---

## Marcus, 26 — Game-Day Casual

**Motivation:** Wants high-energy, zero-cost fun to supplement his occasional stadium visit. Driven by immediate, tangible food/merch prizes.

**Friction Point:** Low attention span. Easily distracted by live play; won't open complex forms or read heavy guidelines.

## The Henderson Family — Social Section Fan

**Motivation:** Enjoys communal experiences, seating section rivalries, and bragging rights. Uses predictions as pre-game icebreakers with kids.

**Friction Point:** Relies on group notifications. If the prediction is too solitary, they lose interest post-kickoff.

## Dave, 44 — Loyal Attendance Strategist

**Motivation:** Season ticket holder. Meticulously tracks weather, opponent hype, and historical gates to lock in highly scientific exact-attendance guesses.

**Friction Point:** Needs historical stats and trend vectors inside the app to keep repeated play intellectually stimulating.

---

## Design checklist for new components/features/pages

Before shipping something new, confirm it works for all three personas:

- [ ] **Marcus** — Can he participate in a few seconds with no reading/setup? Is there a clear, immediate reward?
- [ ] **Henderson Family** — Is it shareable/social? Does it work as a group or section-level activity, not just solo?
- [ ] **Dave** — Does it respect depth? Does it expose stats/history rather than dumbing the mechanic down?
- [ ] Does it preserve the eligibility model (in-venue only, one verified entry per game, free)?
