# 04 — Sponsor Value Loop

Brand engagement through native, high-anticipation live stadium reveals.

## Core Sponsor Job

> "Associate our brand natively with moments of peak crowd anticipation and reward without disrupting the actual gameplay flow."

## Sponsor Jobs

### Non-Disruptive Native Placements
Deploy logo integrations on digital receipt cards, push notifications, and map overlays rather than standard banners.

### The 'Official Reveal' Spotlight
Own the highly-anticipated official attendance announcement on the main scoreboard during the 3rd quarter.

### Verified Lead Conversion
Offer opting-in participants high-value sponsor perks (coupons, free coffee, match gear) on near-miss entries.

## Sponsorship Boundaries

- Absolutely no purchase necessary
- HIPAA/GDPR-compliant data sharing
- Frictionless 1-tap claim flow

---

## Design checklist for new components/features/pages

- [ ] Does it place sponsor branding natively (receipts, notifications, overlays) rather than as a disruptive banner/ad?
- [ ] Does it protect the "Official Reveal" as the peak-anticipation moment, rather than compete with it?
- [ ] If it offers a sponsor perk, is claiming it a 1-tap, no-purchase-necessary flow?
- [ ] Does any data shared with/by a sponsor stay HIPAA/GDPR-compliant?
